CASE STUDY
PRODUCT PAGE CRO

Alpino Foods

Turning a single product page into a complete purchase decision.

Product-page-focused CRO work aimed at removing hesitation between "interested" and "in cart" — clarity, trust, and offer presentation for a subscription-style product.

BEFORE WE STARTED
Before We Started

The product page had the ingredients of a good offer, but they weren't arranged to make the decision easy. The core value of the product wasn't stated plainly up front, the subscription option sat next to the one-time purchase without a clear reason to choose it, and proof elements existed but sat away from the add-to-cart button, where hesitation actually happens.

There was also no structured way to increase order value — every visitor defaulted to the smallest possible purchase because nothing on the page suggested otherwise.

KEY GROWTH LESSONS
What This Project Taught Us
✦A confusing offer costs more sales than a weak design.
✦Subscriptions need to be sold on their own terms, not as an afterthought.
✦Trust has to appear right where the decision is made — not just in the footer.
✦Average order value grows from structure, not just discounts.

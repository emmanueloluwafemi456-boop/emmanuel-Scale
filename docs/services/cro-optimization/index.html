THE PROBLEM
Why Traffic Alone Won't Grow Your Store
Most store owners respond to slow sales by spending more on ads, when the real problem is a leaking funnel — visitors arriving and leaving without buying because of friction they never notice.
⚠
Confusing Checkout Flow
Extra steps, unclear shipping costs, and forced account creation cause visitors to abandon their cart at the finish line.
⚠
Weak Product Pages
Missing information, poor images, and no urgency give visitors no reason to buy right now.
⚠
No A/B Testing
Changes are made based on opinion, not data, so there's no way to know what actually improves conversions.
⚠
Ignoring Mobile Friction
Small tap targets, slow load times, and awkward forms on mobile lose the majority of your traffic.
THE SOLUTION
How Emmanuel Optimizes Differently
Instead of guessing what to change, I use funnel data, heatmaps, and structured A/B testing to find exactly where visitors drop off and fix it with proven, tested changes.
✦THE APPROACH
I diagnose your funnel with real data, then run structured tests to implement only the changes proven to increase conversions — not opinions or best guesses.
1
Funnel Audit
Full audit of your store's buying journey to identify every friction point.
WHY IT MATTERS
You can't fix what you can't see. A thorough audit reveals exactly where visitors are dropping off before they buy.

Heatmap & Behavior Analysis
Visual analysis of how real visitors interact with your store.
WHY IT MATTERS

A/B Testing Program
Structured testing of headlines, layouts, and offers to validate improvements.
WHY IT MATTERS
Only tested changes are guaranteed improvements. Opinion-based changes can just as easily hurt conversions as help them.
BUSINESS IMPACT
Delivers verified, compounding conversion gains.

Checkout Optimization
Streamlined checkout flow to reduce abandonment at the final step.
WHY IT MATTERS
Checkout is where the most revenue is lost to friction. Small improvements here have an outsized impact on total sales.

THE PROBLEM
Why Generic Templates Cost You Sales
A store that looks like every other Shopify store fails to build trust or stand out. Poor visual hierarchy, inconsistent branding, and cluttered layouts push visitors away before they buy.
⚠
Cookie-Cutter Templates
Unedited themes look identical to thousands of other stores, giving customers no reason to trust or remember your brand.
⚠
Poor Visual Hierarchy
Cluttered pages with no clear focal point confuse visitors and bury your best-selling products.
⚠
Inconsistent Branding
Mismatched colors, fonts, and imagery make your store look unprofessional and unfinished.
⚠
Weak Mobile Layouts
Designs built desktop-first often break on mobile, where most of your traffic actually shops.
THE SOLUTION
How Emmanuel Designs Differently
Instead of tweaking a stock theme, I design every page around your brand identity and a clear conversion path — from first scroll to checkout.

THE PROBLEM
If Your Data Is Wrong, Your Decisions Are Wrong.
Most stores aren't missing data — they're making decisions on data that's incomplete, duplicated, or quietly broken.
⚠
Broken Meta Pixel
A misfiring or missing pixel means Meta optimizes toward the wrong signals, and ad spend follows bad data.
⚠
Incorrect GA4 Events
Misconfigured events report activity that doesn't match what's actually happening in your store.
⚠
Missing Purchase Events
If purchases aren't tracked properly, you can't see which channels are actually driving revenue.
⚠
Duplicate Conversions
Double-counted purchases inflate performance numbers and lead to decisions based on numbers that were never real.
⚠
Untracked Checkout Behavior
Without visibility into checkout, you can't see exactly where paying customers are dropping off.
⚠
Poor Attribution
When you can't tell which channel actually drove the sale, budget gets allocated based on guesswork.
⚠
Missing UTM Structure
Without consistent UTM tagging, campaign performance blends together and becomes impossible to compare.
⚠
Incomplete Customer Journey Data
Gaps in the journey make it impossible to see the full path from first click to purchase.
⚠
Ads Reporting Numbers That Don't Match Shopify
When ad platforms and Shopify disagree, you're left not knowing which number to trust.

THE PROBLEM
Why Most Dropshipping Stores Fail
Most dropshipping stores fail not because of marketing, but because of the product itself — saturated niches, unreliable suppliers, and razor-thin margins that can't survive ad costs.
⚠
Saturated Product Picks
Copying trending products everyone else is selling means competing on price alone, with no differentiation.
⚠
Unreliable Suppliers
Slow shipping times and inconsistent quality from unvetted suppliers destroy customer trust and generate refunds.
⚠
Thin Profit Margins
Products priced without accounting for ad costs, fees, and returns leave no room for profitable scaling.
⚠
No Sourcing Strategy
Sourcing one product at a time with no system means constantly starting over when a product dies.
THE SOLUTION
How Emmanuel Sources Differently
Instead of chasing trending products, I run a structured research and vetting process to find products with real demand, healthy margins, and supplier reliability.

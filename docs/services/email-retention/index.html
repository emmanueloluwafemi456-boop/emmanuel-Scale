THE PROBLEM
Why Most Stores Leave Revenue on the Table
Most Shopify stores rely entirely on paid ads to drive every sale, ignoring the customers who already showed interest — abandoned carts, past buyers, and subscribers who never hear from the brand again.
⚠
No Abandoned Cart Recovery
Visitors who add to cart and leave are never followed up with, losing easily recoverable revenue.
⚠
One-and-Done Customers
Past buyers never hear from the brand again, missing the highest-converting audience for repeat sales.
⚠
Generic Email Blasts
Sending the same email to everyone ignores buying behavior and results in poor open and click rates.
⚠
No Retention Strategy
Without a plan to keep customers coming back, every sale requires new ad spend to acquire again.
